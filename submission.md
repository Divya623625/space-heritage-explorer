# Space Heritage Explorer: NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026

**Challenge:** Abandoned but not Forgotten: Storytelling about NASA's Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars
**Team:** Beyond Earth Solo (local event: Universal Event)

## Summary
Space Heritage Explorer is an interactive website that tells the stories of equipment left behind on the Moon and Mars, from the Apollo 11 descent stage to the Ingenuity helicopter. Visitors can browse objects as cards or follow them on a timeline, filter by world, search by name or mission, and open a detail view with the object's purpose, landing site, last contact and story.

## What it does
- Shows the Moon and Mars as draggable 3D globes with a pin at each object's landing site.
- Tells the story of nine abandoned objects (4 on the Moon, 5 on Mars) in order, from Surveyor 3 in 1967 to Ingenuity in 2024, with a Start the story walkthrough.
- Charts how long each object operated, from arrival to last contact.
- Lets visitors search, share a link to any object, and open NASA's image library for photos.

## How we built it
Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no frameworks, using the canvas for the globes and star field, so it loads fast and works on any device. It is keyboard accessible and responsive.

## Why it matters
These objects are the first archaeology beyond Earth. Telling their stories builds public appreciation for the history of exploration and for protecting these sites.

## Data and credits
Mission facts are based on NASA mission pages and NASA's image library (images.nasa.gov). NASA imagery used must be credited to NASA and the relevant mission team.
